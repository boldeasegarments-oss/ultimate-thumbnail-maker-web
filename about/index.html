Our Editorial Purpose

Many mobile creators produce videos entirely from their smartphones without access to desktop graphic design software. Our mission is to publish accurate, transparent, and actionable guides that help creators design clear, high-contrast video covers on Android.

We emphasize realistic, proven design principles:

  • Clarity over complexity: A thumbnail should communicate its core topic within 500 milliseconds.
  • Legibility on mobile: More than 70% of video browsing happens on small displays; we prioritize bold typography and high contrast.
  • Truth in presentation: We encourage creators to make thumbnails that accurately represent their video content.
