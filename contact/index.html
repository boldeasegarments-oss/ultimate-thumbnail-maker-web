How to Report an Issue Effectively

To help resolve your technical inquiry as fast as possible, please provide the following details when writing to support:

  • ✓
    Device & OS: Your Android device model and operating system version (e.g., Samsung Galaxy S24, Android 14).
  • ✓
    App Version: The installed version of Ultimate Thumbnail Maker (found in app settings or Play Store listing).
  • ✓
    Reproduction Steps: A brief description of what you were trying to do (e.g., editing text, applying background removal).
  • ✓
    Error Details: Any error message or unexpected behavior observed on screen.
