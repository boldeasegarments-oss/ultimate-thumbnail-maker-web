Tips for better thumbnails

Creating thumbnails that stand out requires attention to clarity, contrast, and subject focus.

  • ✓ Give the viewer one clear focal point.
  • ✓ Keep wording short and easy to read on a phone.
  • ✓ Use contrast between text, subject, and background.
  • ✓ Avoid placing important details near the edges.
  • ✓ Make the thumbnail accurately represent the content of the video.
  • ✓ Check the design at a small size before publishing.
